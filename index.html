Sea Wall

by Simon Stephens

January 30 · Kent, Ohio

Alex is a photographer. He loves his wife, Helen, his young daughter, Lucy, and his father-in-law, Bob, a retired soldier with a house in the south of France and a habit of arguing about God. Over one quiet, unbroken conversation, Alex tells you about a summer by the sea, the wall that drops away into the deep, and the moment that changed everything. A short, intimate one-man play about love, grief, and what we are left holding.
